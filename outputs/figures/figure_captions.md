# Descriptive figure captions

**Figure 1. Temporal dynamics of e-bike choice at classic docks, January 2025 to October 2026.** Dock-to-dock trips (6,812,658). (a) Daily trips by bike type (7-day centered mean). (b) Daily e-bike share (points) and its 7-day mean (line) for members and casual riders. Grey shading = January 2025, before the 5 February 2025 price change and excluded from model training; teal shading = 2026 test period. Numbered markers: (1) 5 Feb 2025 price increase; (2) early July 2025 $99 membership offer and e-bike cap; (3) 16 March 2026 price change and fleet expansion (see divvy_policy_timeline.csv). (c) Calendar of daily e-bike share (week columns, weekday rows); boxed weeks contain an event. (d) E-bike share by start hour on weekdays and weekends. (e) Change in e-bike share between the same months of 2025 and 2026 (percentage points, pp).

**Figure 2. Who rides which bike, how far and in what conditions.** (a) Flows from year to rider type to bike type (random 60,000 trips; band width ∝ trips). (b) Cumulative distribution of straight-line trip distance by bike type for members (solid) and casual riders (dashed), capped at 8 km; dotted lines mark medians. (c) E-bike share by 4 °C temperature band in 2025 and 2026 with Wilson 95% bands; marker area is proportional to trips (bands with fewer than 300 trips omitted). (d) E-bike share by distance band and start hour in 2026 (cells with fewer than 200 trips blank). (e) E-bike share in 2025 and 2026 under thirteen trip conditions; numbers give the change in percentage points. The rise is present in every condition, which anticipates the decomposition in Figure 13.

**Figure 3. Spatial patterns of e-bike choice.** Origin census tracts with at least 200 dock-to-dock trips in both years are colored; other land tracts are gray (open-water tracts removed). (a) E-bike share in 2026. (b) Change from 2025 to 2026 in percentage points (pp). (c) Bivariate map of the 2026 casual-rider share (blue axis) and e-bike share (red axis), each in tract terciles; dark purple = high on both. (d) Stations with at least 100 trips in 2026 (marker area ∝ trips, color = e-bike share); the square marks the area enlarged in (e), a 7 km window around the Loop. (f) Stations with at least 1,500 trips in each year and the largest rises and falls in e-bike share. Projection: Web Mercator; the scale bar is corrected for latitude.

**Figure 4. When riders choose e-bikes.** E-bike share of dock-to-dock trips by day of week and start hour for members (a-c) and casual riders (d-f): 2025, 2026 and the change in percentage points (pp). Share panels use a common color scale.

**Figure 5. Trip profile.** One-way dock-to-dock trips of 1-180 min and below 40 km/h. (a, b) Rainclouds of straight-line distance and speed for 2,500 random trips per bike × rider group (capped at 8 km and 25 km/h; box = interquartile range). (c) E-bike share by distance × speed bin (cells with fewer than 150 trips blank): at a given distance, faster trips are mostly e-bike trips. (d) Duration density by group (log scale). (e, f) E-bike share by distance and calendar month for members and casual riders in 2025 (dashed) and 2026 (solid); bands are Wilson 95% intervals. Speed and duration are descriptive only and are never model features, because they are only known after the bike is chosen.

**Figure 6. Weather and trip context.** (a) E-bike share (%) by temperature and precipitation; (b) by temperature and start hour (cells with fewer than 300 or 200 trips blank). (c) Each point is a day (area ∝ trips; days with at least 200 trips); lines are means of 3 °C bands: at the same temperature, 2026 days have higher e-bike shares than 2025 days. (d, e) E-bike share by wind speed and temperature for members and casual riders in 2025 (dashed) and 2026 (solid) with Wilson 95% bands. (f) E-bike share by trip context, from 2025 (open circle) to 2026 (arrow tip), for members and casual riders.

**Figure 7. Neighborhood gradients.** (a) E-bike share in 2026 (%) by decile of seven origin-tract characteristics (ACS 2023 5-year; LEHD LODES 2022 for jobs); (b) change from 2025 to 2026 in percentage points. (c) Tracts with at least 200 trips in both years: median income against 2026 e-bike share; marker area ∝ 2026 trips, color = distance to the Loop; the line joins trip-weighted means of income octiles. (d) Tract e-bike share in 2025 against 2026 (dashed line = no change; shaded = increase); color = share of car-free households.

**Figure 8. Correlation among candidate model features.** Spearman correlations on a random sample of 200,000 trips, ordered by hierarchical clustering of 1 − |ρ|. Pairs with |ρ| ≥ 0.7 are listed in Table 3 and guide feature reduction before modeling.
